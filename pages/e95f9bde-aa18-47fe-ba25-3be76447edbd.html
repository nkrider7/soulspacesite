📖 4. An Original Story: The Chef of the Forgotten Sea

Chapter 01 – The Restaurant Beyond the Horizon

The sun slowly disappeared beneath the endless ocean.

Somewhere in the New World, a mysterious island appeared on the horizon.

It wasn't recorded on any map.

An ancient restaurant stood on its shore, surrounded by enormous waves and strange sea creatures.

Inside the restaurant, an old chef was preparing a meal using ingredients nobody had ever seen before.

Sanji stepped inside.

He looked around and noticed a strange symbol carved into the wooden wall.

It was an ancient map of four oceans.

The old chef turned toward him.

"So, you're the cook searching for the All Blue?"

Sanji adjusted his tie and smiled.

"That's right. And I won't stop until I find it."

The old chef placed a mysterious golden ingredient on the table.

"Then prove that you're worthy of discovering the greatest kitchen in the world."

Suddenly, the entire island began shaking.

A gigantic sea creature emerged from the ocean.

Sanji rolled up his sleeves.

Flames appeared around his leg.

"Looks like dinner just arrived."

🔥 His journey toward the All Blue had entered a new chapter.
